## fig1 — Margin of safety vs e/D

![Margin of safety vs e/D](fig1_margin_vs_eD.svg)

All three failure modes against edge distance ratio, for the 7075-T651 straight lug
(D = 26.8 mm, t = 25 mm) under P = 284,686 N.

- Shear-out is strongly geometry-dependent and crosses zero at **e/D = 1.201**
- Bearing is **flat at +0.216**, because bearing area is `D*t` and neither varies in this sweep
- The two cross at **e/D = 1.353** — below it shear-out governs, above it bearing does
- Net section never governs here, a consequence of the `w = 2e` parametrisation
  (see §10 of the results document)

## fig2 — FE response

Two panels. Left: max total deformation against the closed-form shank stretch
`P*L/(A*E)`. The ratio is **1.970, 1.968, 2.002** at e/D = 1.5, 1.8 and 2.0 —
constant to 1.7%, which is what makes it usable as a cross-check. The two
plastically dominated cases do not follow it, as expected.

Right: peak von Mises across the sweep, flat within 6% while the shear-out margin
swings from -0.002 to +1.140. That flatline is the direct observable consequence of
bearing governing. The e/D = 1.0 point is marked as discarded — it requires 37%
plastic strain, far beyond the material's ductility (see §7).

*PNG and SVG for this figure are in the repository release assets or can be
regenerated with the script.*

## fig3 — Open item: plastic strain vs peak stress consistency

![Plastic strain consistency check](fig3_plastic_strain_check.svg)

**This figure documents an unresolved problem, deliberately.**

The bilinear material card implies `epsilon_p = (sigma - 469) / 760` at any yielded
point. Measured plastic strain matches that to 0.6% at e/D = 1.0, but falls to
0.18x of the implied value at e/D = 1.8 and 2.0. The disagreement is monotonic with
plastic zone size.

Working hypothesis: nodal averaging over partly-yielded elements depresses reported
plastic strain more than it depresses reported stress. **Not yet confirmed.** Until
it is, peak stresses for e/D >= 1.2 are treated as indicative rather than
quantitative. None of the conclusions in the results document depend on them.

Resolution path is recorded in §9 of the results document.
